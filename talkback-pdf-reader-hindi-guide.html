Accessibility guide - Hinglish

TalkBack se PDF kaise padhein?

Android par TalkBack ke saath PDF kholne par kabhi text padh jata hai, kabhi page dikhne ke bawajood kuchh sunai nahi deta. Iska matlab hamesha screen reader kharab hai, aisa nahi. Kai PDFs sirf scan ki hui image hoti hain. TalkBack ko padhne ke liye accessible text chahiye.

PDF mein text hai ya sirf scan?

Apne PDF reader mein koi lafz search karke dekhein, ek sentence select karein, ya TalkBack se text par focus le jaane ki koshish karein. Agar kuchh bhi select ya search nahi hota, page image-only ho sakta hai. Yeh pehla ishara hai, pakka diagnosis nahi. Text hone ke baad bhi reading order, headings ya form labels ki dikkat ho sakti hai.

Scan padhne ke liye OCR

OCR image mein likhe lafzon ko machine-readable text mein badalne ki koshish karta hai. OCR ke baad original page se naam, numbers, Hindi/Urdu text aur tables zaroor milayein. Sirf searchable PDF ban jana accessibility ki guarantee nahi. Sahi heading aur reading order bhi chahiye. PDF kyun nahi padhta, iski detailed English guide bhi dekhein.

Lamba text TalkBack se kaise padhein?

TalkBack reading controls mein Words, Headings ya Links chun kar lambe page mein aage badh sakte hain. Kuchh phones par teen ungli se left/right swipe controls badalta hai; dusre phones par ek ungli se up-then-down ya down-then-up gesture kaam karta hai. Control chunnne ke baad ek ungli se neeche swipe agle item par, upar swipe pichhle item par le jata hai. Controls aur gestures phone aur settings par depend kar sakte hain. TalkBack controls ki English guide dekhein.

Reshu PDF Reader ka haal

SR Studio, TalkBack users ke liye Reshu PDF Reader bana raha hai, jismein PDF sunne, navigate karne aur padhne ki jagah yaad rakhne par focus hai. App abhi development aur testing mein hai; public download ya invite milna guaranteed nahi. Android 7 ya usse naye version ke liye testing chal rahi hai. Visually impaired logon ke liye accessible use ko bina ads aur bina charge rakhne ka irada hai; baaki users ke future features ke baare mein abhi final vaada nahi. Testing ka invite maangna ho to homepage ka form bharein. Sahi email dein; invitation milne par link usi email par bheja jayega. Feedback zaroor dein, kyunki aapka test is app ko behtar banane mein madad karega.
