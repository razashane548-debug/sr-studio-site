Donate / Support

Every SR Studio app is free and ad-free, with no paid features: Reshu PDF Reader, Reshu Zone, Status Keeper and everything else we ship. Donations are optional; no donation is required to download or use any app. Donations support app development and improvements for visually impaired users. A donation does not buy a feature, a custom build or a delivery date.
