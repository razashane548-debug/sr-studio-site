Reshu support and FAQ

Install and update

Download only with the official Reshu button. Android 6.0 or later is required. Allow installation from your browser or file app only if you trust this official download, then turn that permission off when no longer needed. Keep Play Protect on and scan first. Read the full Play Protect guidance before changing scanning settings.

Should I uninstall before updating?

No. Install the official matching-signature APK over the existing app. Uninstalling can remove local app data. Back up important records first; an update is not a backup.

Is Reshu free?

The current public v0.3.13-r8 APK is free and ad-free with no paid features. No donation is required.

Does every PDF read aloud?

No. Scans may contain images, and PDF reading order can be wrong. OCR can help but may make mistakes. See how to check a PDF and camera and OCR guide.

Can I use offline voices and translation?

Offline speech depends on a compatible installed voice. Online voices can send text to the speech provider. Translation supports English, Hindi and Urdu after consented model downloads; Punjabi translation is not supported. Translation guide.

Does backup include my book files?

No. Encrypted library and bookmark metadata backup needs a separate recovery key. Keep your original book files and recovery key safe. Cloud-folder support depends on your Android file provider.

What if audio export fails?

On some low-storage phones, audio export may fail. Check available space and keep the original document. Cancellation can wait for an encoding stage, with cleanup taking up to 30 seconds. Report the error with your app version, phone model, Android version and steps. No fix date is promised here.

How do I report a problem?

Use Contact and "Connect on WhatsApp". Include your app version, phone model, Android version, steps and exact error. Do not send private books, passwords or recovery keys. For a suspected vulnerability, use our security reporting guidance.
