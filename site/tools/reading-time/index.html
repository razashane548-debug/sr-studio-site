Reading time calculator

Estimate how long a text might take to read aloud. Enter a word count and a speech pace. You do not need to upload your PDF or paste its contents.



How the estimate works

Minutes = word count divided by words per minute. For example, 3,000 words at 150 words per minute takes about 20 minutes, before pauses.

The pace is your assumption, not a measurement of Reshu or TalkBack. Languages, punctuation, OCR errors, navigation, voice and pauses can change the real time. A phone speech-rate slider is not a words-per-minute setting. Time a short passage to choose a pace that fits you.

This calculator runs in your browser. Inputs are not sent by the calculator or saved. The website's general analytics policy still applies.
