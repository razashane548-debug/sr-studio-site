About SR Studio

Updated October 13, 2026.

SR Studio is an independent Indian software brand. It is founded and run under the SR Studio name - the founder's personal name is intentionally not published.

SR Studio builds accessible Android apps for visually impaired users and for everyone. Our current public app is Reshu PDF Reader, a free, ad-free Android pre-release focused on reading and listening to documents.

What you can download

The official public download is Reshu v0.3.13-r8. We distribute its APK directly from this website, not a Google Play listing. Check the version, requirements, known limits and privacy information before installing.

Help without guesswork

Our guides explain scanned PDFs, speech voices, translation and privacy choices. Results can vary by phone, document and installed voice. We do not promise that every PDF will read correctly.
