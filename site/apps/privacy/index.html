Current app: Reshu PDF Reader v0.3.16

This summary describes version 0.3.16. It is based on APK/source checks, not a packet-level network audit. If you run an older public version, see the historical summaries below.

The plain version

Nothing you read or write leaves your phone. Your books, reading position, bookmarks, notes, vocabulary, voice notes and settings are stored on your device. With default settings the app makes only one kind of network contact on its own: a small update check. Pronunciation data is bundled inside the app, and translation-model downloads happen only when you ask for them. Everything else that uses the network is a separate choice you make, and each one is OFF by default.

What stays on your device

Books you open are read in place through Android's file picker. OCR for scanned pages runs on your device (Tesseract). Translation between English, Hindi and Urdu runs on your device once its model is downloaded. Pronunciation reports and feedback drafts stay on the phone unless you choose to share them. Encrypted backups contain library and bookmark metadata, never the book files, and go only to the location you choose; without your recovery key nobody can read an encrypted backup.

The one automatic contact: update check

The app checks a small version file on this website to see whether an update exists. The hosting service (GitHub) receives ordinary network information, like any website visit. No book text, title or reading activity is included. If you choose to update, the APK downloads from GitHub.

Choices you can turn on - each OFF by default

  • Premium online voice. If you pick the premium voice, the text currently being read is sent to Microsoft's speech service to create the audio. Offline voices never send reading text anywhere.
  • Translation-model downloads. When you first use a translation language, its model downloads from Google (ML Kit). After that, translation runs on device.
  • Push notifications. Opt-in, OFF by default. Uses Firebase Cloud Messaging data-only messages with an in-app inbox.
  • App-usage sharing. Opt-in, OFF by default, and a separate choice from push. Uses Firebase Analytics; events carry no book text, but it is not anonymous.
  • Crash reports. Opt-in, OFF by default. Uses Firebase Crashlytics and sends technical crash details, not book content.
  • Sharing. Exporting, sharing or sending a report sends the files you choose through the app you choose. Reports can include book titles and errors; review them before sharing.
  • System voice search. If you use it, speech is processed by your installed speech provider.

Permissions in v0.3.16

The app has no CAMERA permission: capture uses a system or external camera activity, and Reshu receives the chosen photo. It has no contacts, location, advertising-ID or broad storage permission.

Network, microphone, notification, foreground media/data-sync, wake-lock, boot, vibration, battery-optimization and exact-alarm permissions support related app functions (voice notes, reminders, reading aloud). Optional accessibility-service access for volume-key controls remains separately enabled by you. An SDK-bundled ads-attribution capability is present in the manifest; the app shows no ads and does not use it for advertising.

Your data and deletion

Because your reading data lives on your phone, you hold it: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes it. Optional items you turned on can be turned off individually in Settings; turning off push stops push messages, and turning off usage sharing or crash reports stops new data being sent. Encrypted backups can be deleted wherever you chose to store them. Questions or deletion requests: contact SR Studio below.

Current app: Reshu Zone v0.4.13

This summary describes public version 0.4.13 of Reshu Zone, the audio game hub for blind players. It is based on source checks, not a packet-level network audit.

The plain version

Your games stay on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. The only network contact the app makes on its own is a small update check to the SR Studio website; the hosting service receives ordinary network information, like any website visit.

Permissions in v0.4.13

Network access (used for the update check) and vibration. No microphone, camera, contacts, location or storage permission.

Choices you make

  • Sharing a game report. The share-report button prepares a report file on your phone; you choose the app and the person to send it to. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Updating. If you choose to update, the APK downloads from the SR Studio website or GitHub.

Historical app summary: Reshu PDF Reader v0.3.5

The following section describes the earlier version only. It does not describe 0.3.16.

Choices are separate and off by default

Push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging data-only messages with an in-app inbox. Push is opt-in and OFF by default. Optional app-usage sharing uses Firebase Analytics, is also OFF by default, and is a separate choice from push. Custom usage events carry no book text, but Firebase Analytics is not anonymous. This version does not use Crashlytics.

Camera, translation and backups

The camera-to-book flow processes OCR on device with Tesseract. Offline translation supports English, Hindi and Urdu, with translation-model downloads only after consent. Punjabi translation is not supported.

Encrypted backups contain library/bookmark metadata, with a separate recovery key and a weekly option. Original book files are NOT included. Backup exports and cloud folders use your chosen Android file provider. Keep the recovery key separately; without it, an encrypted backup cannot be read.

Permissions in v0.3.5

The app has no CAMERA permission: capture uses a system or external camera activity, and Reshu receives the chosen photo. It has no broad storage, contacts, location or advertising-ID permission.

Network, microphone, notification, foreground media/data-processing, wake-lock, boot, vibration, battery-optimization and biometric permissions support related app functions. Firebase push permission is present for opt-in messaging. An SDK-bundled Play install-referrer attribution capability is also present; this does not make the app anonymous or identifier-free. Optional accessibility-service access for volume-key controls remains separately enabled by you.

When data can leave the device

  • Online text-to-speech may send reading text to your installed speech provider. This includes automatic mode when it chooses an online voice. Offline voices avoid online TTS processing.
  • System voice search can process speech through its provider. The microphone is used for voice notes and system voice search.
  • Update checks contact the hosted version file through a GET request; the hosting service receives ordinary network information.
  • Model downloads, chosen document/backup providers and Android OS backup can involve network services.
  • User-directed sharing sends the files you choose. Reports can include book titles, file references, vocabulary and errors. Review them before sharing.
  • If you enable app-usage sharing or push, Firebase services process information for those selected functions. No release-announcement notification was sent for this release.

Do not assume that "local" features mean no information can ever leave the device. Your consent choices, installed voice provider, backup settings and selected file provider all matter.

Historical app summary: Reshu PDF Reader v0.3.4

The following section describes the earlier version only. It does not describe 0.3.5 or later.

This app summary is based on the public APK permissions and source review, not a network-traffic audit. It does not describe unreleased versions.

Stored on your device

The library, reading position, bookmarks, vocabulary, statistics, settings and voice notes are stored locally. Documents are chosen through Android's file picker, which can include a cloud provider. OCR processing is local. This version has no account login, advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Firebase push messaging.

When information can leave your device

  • The update check contacts this site at most once a day. Hosting receives ordinary network information, but no book text is included in that check.
  • Online text-to-speech, including automatic voice selection when an online voice is used, may send text to your installed speech provider. Choose an offline voice to avoid online TTS processing.
  • System voice search may process speech through its provider.
  • Sharing or sending a report sends files you choose. Reports can contain book titles, file references, vocabulary and errors. Review them before sharing.
  • Backup exports go to the location or provider you choose. Android's own cloud backup can also apply; app-lock preferences are excluded.

Permissions

The microphone is used for voice-note recording and system voice search. Optional accessibility-service access is enabled by you for volume-key reading controls. Notification, foreground media-playback, biometric and battery-optimization permissions support their related app functions. The public v0.3.4 manifest has no camera, contacts, location or broad storage permission.

"Stored locally" does not mean no information ever leaves the device. Your installed speech provider, Android backup settings and files you choose to share matter.

Permissions inside an app

Review the app’s permission explanations and Android’s permission controls. Do not assume this website notice covers an app’s voice services or other data handling.

Questions
