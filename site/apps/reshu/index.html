Reshu PDF Reader

Free · No ads · Android

Free app that reads your PDFs aloud in Hindi, Urdu, English and Punjabi

Works with TalkBack. Open any text PDF and listen, page by page. Point the camera at a printed page and Reshu reads that too. Built for blind and low-vision users, and for everyone.

What Reshu can do

Four app languages

English, Hindi, Urdu and Indian Punjabi app text with pa-IN voice matching. Punjabi speech needs a compatible installed voice.

Camera and scanned pages

Camera-to-book flow with on-device OCR for scanned pages. OCR can be wrong; check the result before relying on it.

Offline translation

Translate between English, Hindi and Urdu. Models download only after consent. Punjabi translation is not supported; Hindi-Urdu translation uses English as an intermediate.

Your reading tools

Text notes, chapter navigation, quotes, book ratings, vocabulary export, per-book reading speed and page-based reading goals.

Help and controls

Reading widget, more audio-help topics and app-lock relock settings.

Encrypted metadata backup

Back up and restore library/bookmark metadata with a separate recovery key and weekly option. Original book files are NOT included. Cloud-folder support depends on your Android file provider.

Whole-book audio export

Foreground processing, scanned-page OCR fallback and cancellation handling. Cancellation can wait up to 30 seconds for an encode stage.

Separate privacy choices

Push notifications and optional app-usage sharing are separate choices, both OFF by default. Read the privacy summary before enabling either.

Before you install

This is a public pre-release, not a final release. It is a directly distributed APK, not a Google Play download, so follow the installation guide. Install over your existing Reshu app; do not uninstall first, so you keep your app data.

  • A scanned PDF can contain images instead of readable text. Do not assume every file can be read aloud.
  • Online voices may require an internet connection. Offline voice availability depends on installed speech support.

Known limits

OCR and translation can be wrong; Punjabi and Urdu speech depend on installed voices. Audio-export cancellation may wait up to 30 seconds. A minor “1 books finished” grammar issue remains. The developer reports successful phone checks on his Redmi, but this is not a guarantee for every device, document or installed speech voice.
