In the current public release

Four ways to drive

Free drive on your own, race against computer opponents (2 on easy, 3 on normal, 4 on hard), carry passengers in taxi mode, or tour India route by route.

India Tour (Bharat Yatra)

Six routes across India. Delhi to Jaipur is open first; finish a route to unlock the next. Locked routes explain themselves aloud. The game announces the route start, city milestones and the distance left.

Calm taxi mode

Pick up passengers and drop them at their destination. Traffic gives way, there is no crash penalty and no game over, and guidance repeats so you can play relaxed.

Real sounds, not beeps

Recorded copyright-free effects: engines, horn, crash, whoosh, start and game-over sounds. Music volume and announcement speed are adjustable with a short preview before you decide.

Made for TalkBack

Every screen and every mode works by ear. Announcements are built to finish speaking, not get cut off. Visuals are included for sighted players too.

English, Hindi, Urdu

Play and hear announcements in English, Hindi or Urdu.

Share your session report

Send a formatted PDF report of your game session in one tap, with an optional spoken audio recap you can listen to like a voice note. You can also watch a video replay of your drive.

Installing Reshu Zone and Google Play Protect

Reshu Zone v0.4.13 is a public pre-release distributed directly as an APK. It is not currently published on Google Play. Installing an APK from outside Google Play does not automatically require turning Play Protect off.

Keep Play Protect on and scan first

Download only through the official Reshu Zone download button on this page. Allow Google Play Protect to scan the APK before installing. If it specifically says the app is harmful, do not ignore that warning or turn protection off to bypass it. To report it, use the 'Contact' link in the navigation at the top of this page. Include the exact warning, app version and phone model. Do not send private documents or passwords.

If installation is still blocked

If you choose to temporarily turn off scanning after checking the warning, open Google Play Store > your profile > Play Protect > Settings, then turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect" and attempt the installation. Turning off scanning reduces protection for your device; it does not prove that an APK is safe. Do not install other APKs while scanning is off. Some phones or security settings may not allow this option.

Turn Play Protect back on immediately

After the installation attempt, return to Google Play Store > your profile > Play Protect > Settings and turn "Scan apps with Play Protect" ON again. Do this even if installation fails. Do not leave Play Protect off.
