Reshu Status Saver

In testing

Save statuses, without the grid hunt.

A blind-friendly WhatsApp status saver for Android. Built TalkBack-first: plain lists and buttons instead of ad-heavy grids. Free, no ads, and nothing leaves your phone.

  • StatusIn testing - no public APK yet
  • Latest build0.2.0 (version code 3)
  • FormatAndroid APK (when released)

What it does

Save the statuses you choose

Keep status photos and videos you have already viewed, in your own gallery. You pick what to save; nothing is saved automatically.

TalkBack-first

Plain lists, labelled buttons and spoken feedback. No ad grids, no popups, no traps.

Nothing leaves your phone

An internet permission is bundled with an on-device library and is never used. Nothing leaves your phone.

Your folder, your permission

On newer Android versions you grant access to the statuses folder once, through Android's own folder picker. The app sees only that folder.

Availability

Reshu Status Saver is in testing. There is no public APK to download yet, and no release date has been announced. A public download will appear here only when a release is ready and approved.
