Updated October 8, 2026. For editors, directory reviewers and podcast hosts. No private or unreleased app material is included.

Short description

SR Studio builds accessible Android reading tools. Reshu PDF Reader is a free, ad-free Android pre-release for reading and listening to documents, with scanned-page OCR and consent-based offline translation.

Current public facts

  • App: Reshu PDF Reader v0.3.13-r8, version code 15.
  • Android 6.0 or later; APK 75,395,258 bytes (71.9 MiB).
  • Direct APK distribution from our website, not a verified Google Play listing.
  • English, Hindi, Urdu and Indian Punjabi app text. Compatible speech voices depend on the phone; Punjabi translation is not supported.
  • OCR, translation and reading order can be wrong. This is a pre-release, not an all-device-tested final release.
