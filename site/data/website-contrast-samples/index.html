Website contrast samples dataset

Three selected homepage color pairs from SR Studio's October 8, 2026 public website accessibility report, available as CSV. These are measured samples, not an app test, a complete state audit or a WCAG certification.

Download the CSV (three rows)

What is included

  • Body text: #1b2a2e on #fbfaf6, contrast ratio 14.19:1.
  • Lead text: #46585c on #fbfaf6, contrast ratio 7.15:1.
  • Primary button text: #ffffff on #0b5f5c, contrast ratio 7.48:1.

Method and provenance

The published report used computed homepage colors and the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Its environment was automated Chromium 154.0.8037.57 on Linux. The CSV preserves the published rounded ratios and the original check date, October 8, 2026. It was prepared for download on October 13, 2026; that is not a new measurement date.

Columns: sample, foreground color, background color, contrast ratio, checked date and scope. Hex colors are sRGB. Contrast ratios have no unit. Background gradients, hover states, every image and every text size are not covered.

Limits and reuse

These historical samples do not prove that the current site, APK, or every interactive state meets accessibility standards. No screen-reader speech session was run for that report. Public download does not create a new open-data licence; the existing site terms apply. Cite the original test report when referring to these measurements.
