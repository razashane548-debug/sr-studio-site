Website privacy

Updated October 7, 2026.

Reading and downloads

This static website has no accounts or advertising. This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views, visits and page performance. It does not use cookies or browser storage for analytics. Cloudflare processes these measurements under its privacy policy. Counts are approximate and may miss visitors whose browser blocks the analytics script. APK downloads are served directly from this website; a click or visit to our download page is not proof that a download finished or the app was installed. Cloudflare hosts the website and may process routine request data under its privacy policy. APK download buttons serve files directly from this website.

Contact

Contact opens WhatsApp, where the contact number may be visible. WhatsApp processes messages under its own policies. Please include your name, email and query, but do not send passwords or sensitive documents.

App privacy is separate

This notice covers the website. Review the Reshu privacy and permissions summary before enabling optional app features.
