Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026.

SR Studio builds Android apps for blind and low-vision users, and for everyone. This page says what little data our apps and this website use. SR Studio does not sell your data.

Voice and AI features

Some SR Studio apps can respond to spoken commands or use AI. When you use those features, what you say or type is processed so the app can do what you asked. Some of this processing is done by outside AI services, under their own terms and privacy policies. We tell you in the app which features need an internet connection.

Accessibility use

Apps that help you use your phone can read what is on screen and act on it, using Android accessibility features. They do this to carry out your requests. This access is used for the app to work, not to advertise to you.

Reshu PDF Reader

The current public Reshu release is v0.3.13-r8. Reading records are mainly stored on your device, but the choices below can send data outside it. Reshu has no ads.

Speech, OCR and translation

The phone's text-to-speech service reads text. Online speech services, including an optional Microsoft online premium voice, may receive the text you ask them to speak. Use an installed on-device voice for sensitive text. OCR and translation processing run on your phone; translation models download after consent. System voice search, your chosen file provider and deliberate sharing can also send information outside the phone under those services' policies.

Optional app usage sharing

Usage sharing is a separate choice, off by default. If enabled, Firebase Analytics receives app-added app-open, feature-use, import-result and export-result events with enumerated values, plus automatic usage and technical data and an app-instance identifier. It is not anonymous. App-added events do not include book names or text, filenames, recordings, contacts, account details or location. Turning it off disables future collection and resets analytics state; it does not promise deletion of data already uploaded.

Optional crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics is included in this release, with separate consent off by default. If you enable "Send crash reports", it can send technical failure, device and Android details and an installation identifier. These reports are not anonymous. Turning crash reports off deletes unsent reports; this is not a promise to erase reports already sent.

Notifications

Reading playback controls are distinct from optional announcements. Firebase Cloud Messaging announcements and the app's notification inbox require a separate opt-in; FCM auto-initialization is off until consent.

Backups and sharing

Encrypted backup saves library references, reading positions, snippets, bookmarks, notes and highlights, not original PDFs or audio. Restore requires the matching recovery-key file. Cloud-folder use depends on your chosen Android provider; anyone with both the backup and key can read its metadata. Keep them separate and do not send them to support. The separate legacy reshu-backup.json export is not encrypted.

Android device backup is enabled and can transfer app data through the device's backup service. App-lock preferences, push/privacy preferences, weekly-backup keys and Firebase measurement preferences/database are excluded. Android settings and provider policies also apply.

See the backup and recovery-key guide and plain-language privacy choices. These facts describe the current release; they are not a promise that all device or service settings behave identically.

Payments, donations and delivery

If you pay or donate, or ask for a personal build, we keep a record so we can deliver the app and settle any dispute: who paid, when, how much, the device ID you gave us and which build we sent. We do not sell or share this record for marketing.

This website

This website has no accounts or advertising. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for approximate page views, visits and page performance, without analytics cookies or browser storage. Cloudflare hosts this website and may process routine request data under its privacy policy. APK downloads are served directly from this website. See the website privacy notice for details.

Contact

For questions or to ask us to delete your records, write to contact@srstudio.dpdns.org. Please do not send passwords or sensitive documents.
