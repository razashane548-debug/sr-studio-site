Invite requests

When you submit a request, we receive the name, email address, project selection and reason you enter. We use these details to review your request and, if appropriate, follow up about testing. Submitting does not guarantee an invitation.

Our form is delivered by FormSubmit, which sends the submission to the SR Studio team's inbox. FormSubmit says it retains submissions in its archive for 30 days. Avoid entering passwords or sensitive personal information.

Contact and removal

For questions or to ask us to remove an invite request from our inbox, send a WhatsApp message with your name, email ID and query or complaint. Connect on WhatsApp. Your message opens WhatsApp, where the contact number may be visible.

This website is hosted on Netlify, which may process routine request and usage data under its own policies. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
